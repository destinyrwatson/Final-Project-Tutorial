# Why Digitization Matters

[Back: How Digitization Works](how_digitization_works.md) | [Next: Everyday Examples](everyday_examples.md)

Digitization is the foundation of modern digital technology.

## Key Benefits
- **Storage:** Large amounts of information can be stored digitally or in the cloud.
- **Sharing:** Digital files can be copied and shared instantly across the world.
- **Preservation:** Historical documents, films, and music can be saved for future generations.
- **Efficiency:** Digital information is easier to edit, search, and organize.

Without digitization, modern communication and technology would not be possible.
