# Understanding Digitization: Turning the Physical World into Digital Data

**Author:** Destiny Watson

## Summary
This tutorial explains the digital concept of digitization, which is the process of converting physical or analog information into digital formats that computers can store, process, and share. It covers how digitization works, why it matters, and real-world examples people encounter every day.

## Target Audience
This tutorial is intended for beginners and undergraduate students with little to no technical background. It is suitable for anyone who uses digital technology and wants to understand how physical information becomes digital.
