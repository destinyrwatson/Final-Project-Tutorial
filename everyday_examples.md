# Everyday Examples of Digitization

[Back: Why Digitization Matters](why_digitization_matters.md) | [Next: Conclusion](conclusion.md)

Digitization happens all around us in daily life.

## Common Examples

| Real-World Item        | Digital Version     | Format Example |
|------------------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Handwritten notes      | PDF document        | .pdf           |
| Old photograph         | Digital image       | .jpg, .png     |
| Vinyl record           | Audio file          | .mp3, .wav     |
| X-ray film             | Digital scan        | .dcm           |

These examples show how physical items become usable in digital systems.
