# How Digitization Works

[Back: What Is Digitization?](what_is_digitization.md) | [Next: Why Digitization Matters](why_digitization_matters.md)

Digitization happens through two main steps: **sampling** and **quantization**.

## Sampling
Sampling is the process of taking measurements of the original information at specific points.

**Example:**  
When recording audio, a microphone captures sound waves thousands of times per second. CD-quality audio uses 44,100 samples per second.

## Quantization
Quantization assigns a numeric value to each sample. These values are stored in binary form using 0s and 1s.

Together, sampling and quantization allow computers to recreate digital versions of real-world information.
