# What Is Digitization?

[Back to Home](README.md) | [Next: How Digitization Works](how_digitization_works.md)

## Definition
Digitization is the process of converting information into a digital format that computers can understand and store. In simple terms, it turns real-world objects or signals into data made of 0s and 1s.

## Examples
- Scanning a paper document creates a digital file such as a PDF or image.
- Recording music turns sound waves into digital audio files like MP3s.
- Taking a photo with a smartphone converts light into digital pixels.

Digitization allows information to exist beyond physical form and be used by digital systems.
